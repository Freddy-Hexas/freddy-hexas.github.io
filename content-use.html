Technical limits

robots.txt and HTML indexing directives communicate preferences to cooperating services. They do not authenticate visitors, prevent downloads, or guarantee compliance. Public GitHub repository and raw-file URLs remain independently accessible. These preferences do not remove existing copies or previously collected training data.

This site immediately disallows all automated crawling. Its HTML pages also declare noindex, nofollow, nosnippet, and noimageindex. A crawler blocked by robots.txt may not see those HTML directives, so previously indexed results may still require a removal request in the search engine's webmaster tools.
