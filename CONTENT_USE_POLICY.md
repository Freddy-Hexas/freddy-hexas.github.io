## Technical limits

`robots.txt` and HTML indexing directives communicate preferences to cooperating services. They do not authenticate visitors, prevent downloads, or guarantee that a service will comply. Public GitHub repository and raw-file URLs remain independently accessible. These preferences do not remove existing copies or previously collected training data.

This site immediately disallows all automated crawling through `robots.txt`. Its HTML pages also declare `noindex`, `nofollow`, `nosnippet`, and `noimageindex`. A crawler blocked by `robots.txt` may not see the HTML directives, so these settings do not guarantee removal of a previously indexed URL. Previously indexed results may require a removal request in the search engine's webmaster tools.
