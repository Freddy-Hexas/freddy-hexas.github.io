Fang Xu

Biomedical Engineering · ShanghaiTech University

I am an undergraduate student in Biomedical Engineering at ShanghaiTech University, working in the Wireless and Smart Bio-electronics (WiSe) Lab with Prof. Ze Xiong.

My research connects neural interfaces, computational design, and machine learning for biomedical signals. I develop tools for organoid electrode-interface design and cross-subject EEG emotion recognition.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Sung Il Park on adaptive wireless power transfer and flexible optoelectronic devices. I expect to graduate in July 2027.
